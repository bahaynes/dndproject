# Rattleguard & The High Fjords

*Player-facing campaign primer — safe to surface on an "About the Campaign" page.*

---

## The Pitch

> You arrived in a majestic ridge city by spelljammer or portal, and promptly found out how expensive rent is when the Mist comes every night and everyone wants permanent safety. Down in the valleys, though, lie the magical crystals that power the rest of the verse—and someone has to collect them.
>
> Welcome to **Rattleguard**, a frontier town perched just below the mistline. Its warding braziers work, mostly. Spirits still take a few people each night, but the pay is good and the miners are friendly. You're with **The Troublesome Company** now—recruited when you ran out of coin and jobs up top. Pick a partner, pick a contract, and remember the only rule: **be back behind the braziers before dark.**

---

## The World: Elevation Is Safety

The High Fjords are a vertical frontier of steep-walled glacial valleys and broad, sunlit plateaus:

* **Above the Mistline (The Ridge Cities):** Safe, crowded, and outrageously expensive. Gleaming city-states crown the high plateaus, connected by sky-docks, portals, and looping ridge-roads.
* **Below the Mistline (The Valleys):** Lush mist-forests, limestone quarries, ruined towns, and crystal veins—completely safe to explore by day, and deadly after dark.
* **The Evening Bells & The Mist:** Every night at sunset, the **Mist** rises out of the valleys up to a predictable line along the fjord walls. Whatever it touches suffocates, and things that don't need to breathe come out to hunt. Fire and warding chalk hold it back; dawn burns it away.

**Travel Scale:** Cutting straight across a fjord valley between neighboring ridges takes **a full day's march**—fast and tempting if you leave at dawn, dangerous if anything slows you down before the evening bells. Taking the safe high-ridge roads around the head of the fjords takes days and heavy Cartel tolls.

## Rattleguard & The Troublesome Company

**Rattleguard** sits on a fortified river-terrace at the mouth of the first fjord, just below the mistline. Its brazier-walls went up recently enough that **no single city-state or syndicate owns it yet**—making it the one place in the region where broke adventurers, fortune-hunters, and smooth talkers can actually move the needle.

**The Troublesome Company** is your hiring outfit: scrappy, underinsured, and pragmatic. With a rotating roster of contractors (~10 on the books, 4 or so heading out on any given morning), the Company matches whoever shows up with a job, a partner, and a fair cut of whatever they haul back before sunset.

## The Factions

### Global Factions
* **The Troublesome Company:** Your contractor outfit in Rattleguard. Fair pay, questionable insurance, and one rule: get back before dark.
* **The Crystal Cartel:** A global merchant syndicate that buys valley crystals, controls the cliff-lifts and river barges, and sells brim-coal brazier fuel at eye-watering markups.
* **The Lamplighters:** The civic and religious order that tends the warding braziers and rings the Sunset Bells. They treat keeping the Mist at bay as sacred duty.

### The Ridge City-States
* **Highspire:** Directly above Rattleguard via cliff-lift and switchbacks. Sky-docks, portals, old money, and the apartments you could no longer afford.
* **Ironstep:** Industrial quarry city-state on the eastern ridge, a full day's valley crossing (or four-day ridge loop) away. Always feuding with Highspire over crystal tariffs.
* **Crow's Ledge:** Cliff-cave smugglers' port perched above the western fjord. Black-market gear, no tariffs, no questions asked.
* **Bellwatch:** Walled abbey city-state on the northern plateau housing pre-Mist archives and the Lamplighters' seminary.

## Frontier Gear (Rattleguard Store)

* **Spirit-Ward Canary (15 gp):** A small yellow canary in a brass cage. Miners swear it stops singing whenever valley spirits draw near.
* **Warding Chalk (5 gp):** Alchemically treated white chalk used to seal doorframes and cellar thresholds against the creeping Mist.
* **Brim-Coal Brazier Oil (10 gp):** Heavy mineral oil that burns hot and bright, pushing back the evening Mist around camp lanterns and river barges.
* **Climber's Kit (25 gp) / Antitoxin (50 gp):** Standard insurance when a valley crossing runs late.

## Table Rules at a Glance

* **3-Month Arc, 4-Player Parties:** Designed for a 10-player roster where any 4 can drop in on a given session.
* **Play Anything:** You arrived by spelljammer or portal—any 5e lineage or class fits right in.
* **Flight & *Water Breathing*:** Getting above the mistline works, though you still need somewhere safe to land and rest (and *Brooms of Flying* are off the table). *Water Breathing* works in an emergency river dive, not as an 8-hour glacial hotel.
