# Rattleguard & The High Fjords

*Player-facing campaign primer — safe to surface on an "About the Campaign" page.*

---

## The Pitch

> You arrived in a majestic ridge city by spelljammer or portal, and promptly found out how expensive rent is when the Mist comes every night and everyone wants permanent safety. Down in the valleys, though, lie the magical crystals that power the rest of the verse—and someone has to collect them.
>
> Welcome to **Rattleguard**, a frontier town perched just below the mistline. Every night the whole town works to keep an unbroken ring of braziers burning. The ring holds, mostly. Spirits still take a few people, but the pay is good and the miners are friendly. You're with **The Troublesome Company** now—recruited when you ran out of coin and jobs up top. Pick a partner, pick a contract, and remember the only rule: **be back behind the braziers before dark.**

---

## The World: Elevation Is Safety

The High Fjords are a vertical frontier of steep-walled glacial valleys and broad, sunlit plateaus:

* **Above the Mistline (The Ridge Cities):** Safe, crowded, and outrageously expensive. Gleaming city-states crown the high plateaus, connected by sky-docks, portals, and looping ridge-roads.
* **Below the Mistline (The Valleys):** Lush mist-forests, limestone quarries, ruined towns, and crystal veins—free of the Mist by day, and deadly after dark. The ruins are towns that stopped holding their ring.
* **The Evening Bells & The Mist:** Every night at sunset, the **Mist** rises out of the valleys up to a predictable line along the fjord walls. Whatever it touches suffocates within minutes, and things that don't need to breathe come out to hunt. Dawn burns it away.
* **Only a ring holds:** The Mist flows around a lone flame. A torch or lantern buys you minutes; an unbroken, fueled, crewed ring of braziers buys a night. That takes a town. There are no waystations and no camping in the valley.

**Travel Scale:** Caravans ride the high ridge-roads (the "fingers") around the head of the fjords, above the Mist, and take days. Cutting straight across a valley between neighboring ridges takes **a full day's march** with no shelter on the way. It's what smugglers and the desperate do.

## Rattleguard & The Troublesome Company

**Rattleguard** sits on a fortified river-terrace at the mouth of the first fjord, just below the mistline. It sits in the thin top of the Mist; deeper towns had it worse. Its brazier-walls went up recently enough that **no single city-state or syndicate owns it yet**—making it the one place in the region where broke adventurers, fortune-hunters, and smooth talkers can actually move the needle.

**The Troublesome Company** is your hiring outfit: scrappy, underinsured, and pragmatic. With a rotating roster of contractors (~10 on the books, 4 or so heading out on any given morning), the Company matches whoever shows up with a job, a partner, and a fair cut of whatever they haul back before sunset.

## The Factions

### Global Factions
* **The Troublesome Company:** Your contractor outfit in Rattleguard. Fair pay, questionable insurance, and one rule: get back before dark.
* **The Crystal Cartel:** A global merchant syndicate that buys valley crystals, runs the ridge-road caravans and cliff-lifts, and sells brim-coal at eye-watering markups to towns that can't let their ring go out.
* **The Lamplighters:** The civic and religious order that runs the night shifts, tends the brazier ring, and rings the Sunset Bells. They treat keeping the Mist at bay as sacred duty.

### The Ridge City-States
* **Highspire:** Directly above Rattleguard via cliff-lift and switchbacks. Sky-docks, portals, old money, and the apartments you could no longer afford.
* **Ironstep:** Industrial quarry city-state on the northeastern ridge, four days away by ridge-road, or a full day's sprint across the valley. Always feuding with Highspire over crystal tariffs.
* **Crow's Ledge:** Cliff-cave smugglers' port on a high western peak the ridge-road never reaches. Black-market gear, no tariffs, no questions asked.
* **Bellwatch:** Walled abbey city-state on the northern plateau housing pre-Mist archives and the Lamplighters' seminary.

## Bramwell's Quartermaster Shed

Standard D&D equipment at list prices: weapons, armor, packs, tools, potions, and a few magic items. There's nothing for sale that lets you survive a night in the Mist.

## Table Rules at a Glance

* **3-Month Arc, 4-Player Parties:** Designed for a 10-player roster where any 4 can drop in on a given session.
* **Play Anything:** You arrived by spelljammer or portal—any 5e lineage or class fits right in.
* **Flight & *Water Breathing*:** Getting above the mistline works, though you still need somewhere safe to land and rest (and *Brooms of Flying* are off the table). *Water Breathing* works in an emergency river dive, not as an 8-hour glacial hotel.
